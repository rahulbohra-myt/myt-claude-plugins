## Earnings

**Framing rule:** this is a marketplace, not a job — no fixed or guaranteed income. Earnings are
driven by supply and demand (the teacher's own time/skill investment vs. student demand). Always
pair any earnings figure with this context; never imply guaranteed income.

**Earnings growth curve** (avg / max, by tenure):

| Stage   | Avg. earnings | Max. earnings |
| ------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| Month 1 | Rs 3,500      | Rs 23,000     |
| Month 3 | Rs 20,000     | Rs 1,00,000   |
| Year 1  | Rs 45,000     | Rs 1,80,000   |
| Year 2  | Rs 60,000     | Rs 2,25,000   |

**Supporting claim:** teachers who stay consistent and actively engage with students typically
cross Rs 1,00,000 in earnings within their first year.

**Top-end data point:** highest recorded teacher income was Rs 2,00,000 in a single month — an
aspirational "top teachers" data point, not a typical outcome.

## Application & Onboarding Process

1. **Application:** Apply → Screening call → Demo session → 5-day training (credentials
   verification, class-flow best practices, profile creation)
2. **Getting started:** Set availability → Students book sessions → Earn and grow (platform/policy
   intro, monthly payment setup)
3. **Ongoing tools & support:** teacher app/dashboard for scheduling, marketing support (MYT brings
   the students), 24x7 live support

**Summary line:** "We bring the students and manage the platform — you focus on teaching."
Most applicants move through the full process within a couple of weeks.
