## SECTION 8: FAQ

**Headline:** Frequently Asked Questions

**1. Is yoga good for neck and shoulder pain?**
Yes. Most neck and shoulder tension comes from tight muscles, poor posture, and stress held in the upper body. Yoga directly addresses all of this through targeted stretching, strengthening, and breath-based relaxation. Unlike a quick stretch or massage, a consistent yoga practice helps correct the underlying posture and movement patterns that cause tension to keep coming back.

**2. I am a beginner. Can I do yoga for neck and shoulder pain?**
Absolutely. A 1-on-1 format is ideal for beginners, especially if you're experiencing pain or stiffness. Your instructor guides you through gentle, beginner-friendly poses, adjusts every movement to your comfort level, and ensures you're practicing safely. Many of our students begin with no prior yoga experience and feel more confident learning with personalized guidance than following online videos.

**3. Can yoga help a frozen shoulder?**
Yes. If you're dealing with frozen shoulder, our instructors can guide you through gentle, gradual movements designed to reduce stiffness and improve shoulder mobility over time. Every session is adapted to your current range of movement, so you're never forced into painful positions.

**4. I sit at a desk all day. Can yoga improve my posture?**
Yes. Poor posture is one of the biggest contributors to neck and shoulder discomfort. Yoga strengthens the muscles that support good posture while releasing tight areas caused by prolonged sitting.

**5. How often should I practice yoga for neck and shoulder tension?**
We recommend 2–3 sessions per week for consistent relief. Your instructor will design a schedule around your pain level, your day-to-day routine, and your goals. Even short, focused sessions can meaningfully reduce tension when done consistently — your instructor will help you find a sustainable rhythm rather than sporadic, intense sessions.
