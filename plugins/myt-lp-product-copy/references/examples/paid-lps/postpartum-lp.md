## SECTION 8: FAQ

**Headline:** Frequently Asked Questions
**Subtext:** Quick answers to commonly asked questions on postpartum yoga
*(Select 5–6 of the 7 options below for the final page — FAQ 1–6 recommended as the priority set)*

**1. When can I start postpartum yoga?**
Most women can begin gentle postpartum yoga as early as 1–2 weeks after a vaginal birth — starting with breathwork and light pelvic floor engagement — and 6–8 weeks after a C-section, with a doctor's clearance. Our instructors are trained to meet you at any stage of recovery. We always work within your healthcare provider's guidelines and will never push you beyond what's safe for your body right now.

**2. Can I do yoga after a C-section?**
Yes, with your doctor's clearance. Our instructors are experienced in C-section recovery and know exactly which poses to avoid and which support scar healing, core reconnection, and gradual strength rebuilding. Your program will be designed specifically around your recovery timeline — no guesswork, no risk of doing the wrong thing at the wrong time.

**3. Will yoga help with my pelvic floor after birth?**
Absolutely. Pelvic floor restoration is one of the most requested areas in our postpartum sessions. Our instructors incorporate diaphragmatic breathing, gentle pelvic floor reconnection, and progressive core work — with live, real-time guidance so you know you're engaging correctly. Unlike YouTube videos, your instructor can actually observe and cue you personally in real time.

**4. Can yoga help with postpartum depression or anxiety?**
Yes. Research supports yoga as an effective complementary practice for managing postpartum depression and anxiety — particularly breathwork, gentle movement, and the sense of consistency a regular practice provides. Our instructors are empathetic and experienced in supporting the emotional as well as physical side of postpartum recovery. *Note: yoga is complementary to, not a replacement for, professional mental health support. We always encourage working alongside your healthcare provider.*

**5. I have diastasis recti. Is postpartum yoga safe for me?**
Yes, with proper guidance. Our postpartum instructors are trained to identify and work safely around diastasis recti (abdominal separation that commonly occurs during pregnancy). We modify or avoid poses that put unsafe pressure on your midline, and focus on targeted reconnection exercises that support healing. Many students see measurable improvement in their diastasis with consistent, properly guided practice.

**6. I've never done yoga before. Can I start postpartum?**
Absolutely. Many of our postpartum students are complete beginners, and the 1-on-1 format is actually ideal for starting from scratch — every pose is explained, demonstrated, and modified for your body. Our instructors are trained to work with all experience levels, and your sessions start as gentle and accessible as you need.

**7 (Alternate). How often should I practice postpartum yoga?**
We recommend starting with 2 sessions per week for early recovery, building to 3 as your energy returns. Your instructor will design a schedule based on your recovery stage and goals. Sessions are flexible — book them during nap time or whenever works for you that week. Consistency matters more than frequency in early postpartum, and your instructor will help you stay on track without burning out.
