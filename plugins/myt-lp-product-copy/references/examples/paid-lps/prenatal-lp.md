### ALL FAQ OPTIONS (Select 5 for final page):

#### FAQ 1: Is prenatal yoga safe for all stages of pregnancy?

Yes! Our certified prenatal yoga instructors are trained to modify every pose for your specific trimester and any pregnancy complications. Whether you're in your first trimester with morning sickness or third trimester preparing for labor, we create a safe practice tailored to your body's needs. We also work with your healthcare provider's recommendations.

---

#### FAQ 2: I've never done yoga before. Can I start during pregnancy?

Absolutely! Many of our students start yoga for the first time during pregnancy. Our instructors specialize in working with beginners and will teach you everything from the basics of breathing to safe stretching techniques. Your practice will be completely personalized to your experience level and comfort.

---

#### FAQ 3: How does prenatal yoga help with labor and delivery?

Prenatal yoga strengthens the muscles you'll use during labor, teaches breathing techniques to manage contractions, and builds stamina for delivery. Our instructors focus on pelvic floor exercises, hip openers, and positions that can ease labor pain. Many moms report shorter labors and feeling more prepared for birth.

---

#### FAQ 4: Can I practice if I have pregnancy complications or bed rest restrictions?

Yes, with your doctor's approval. Our instructors are experienced in adapting practices for various pregnancy conditions including gestational diabetes, preeclampsia, placenta previa, and modified bed rest. We'll work within your healthcare provider's guidelines to create a safe, gentle practice.

---

#### FAQ 5: When should I start prenatal yoga, and can I continue postpartum?

You can start prenatal yoga anytime during pregnancy, though many women begin in the second trimester when energy returns. Our instructors adapt your practice as your pregnancy progresses and can continue supporting you postpartum with recovery-focused yoga to rebuild core strength and relieve new mom tension.

---

#### FAQ 6 (ALTERNATE): What's the difference between online prenatal yoga and in-person classes?

With MyYogaTeacher, you get 1-on-1 attention that's impossible in crowded studio classes — plus the comfort of practicing at home without commuting while pregnant. Your instructor sees you in real-time via Zoom, provides live corrections, and modifies poses specifically for your body. It's more personalized than in-person group classes.

---

#### FAQ 7 (ALTERNATE): How often should I practice prenatal yoga?

Most expecting moms practice 2-3 times per week for best results. Your instructor will recommend a schedule based on your trimester, energy levels, and goals. Sessions are flexible — book them when you're feeling your best, whether that's morning, afternoon, or evening.
