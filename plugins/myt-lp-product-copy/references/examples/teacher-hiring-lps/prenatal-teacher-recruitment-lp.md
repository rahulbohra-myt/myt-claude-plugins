## 9. FAQ

**Section headline:** Frequently Asked Questions

**1. Do I need my own website, marketing, or social media to get students?**
No. MyYogaTeacher brings students to you — you don't need to market yourself, build a following, or chase clients. Your job is to teach; ours is to connect you with students who are looking for a teacher like you.

**2. Is there any fee to apply or train?**
No. Applying, training, and onboarding are completely free.

**3. How and when do I get paid?**
Teachers are paid monthly, based on the sessions they teach.

**4. Is this a fixed-income job?**
Not exactly. MyYogaTeacher is a platform for building your own teaching practice, not a salaried position. We bring you the students — how much you teach and earn is up to you, similar to running your own business.

**5. How many sessions can I expect in a day?**
This varies from teacher to teacher and depends on your availability, the hours you open up for teaching, and student demand. Most teachers start with a few sessions a day and see that grow over time as they build reviews, returning students, and a stronger profile.

**6. How long does the application process take?**
After you apply, expect a screening call, a demo session, and a 5-day training before you start teaching — most applicants move through the full process within a couple of weeks.
