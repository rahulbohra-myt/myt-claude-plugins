# Playbook — Teacher Hiring Landing Page

**Side:** Teacher · **Track:** B — pure conversion (`MYT_Brand_Core.md` §9)
**Status:** Calibrated on one shipped page — `examples/teacher-hiring-lps/prenatal-teacher-recruitment-lp.md`
and Figma `Branding-page-updates` node 10062-5814. Ask for more examples when a new specialization
page comes up.
**Default CTA:** "Apply Now" → **Typeform** (get the campaign's URL at intake) · Trust line:
"Trusted by [N]+ teachers across the globe" (N from Brand Core §8)
**Facts source:** `Teacher_Hiring_Context.md`

## When this applies

Pages that Meta recruitment ads send prospective yoga teachers to — generic ("build your teaching
business with MYT") or a specialization (prenatal, yoga therapy, etc.).

## Category intake (on top of the shared intake)

1. Specialization, or a generic hiring page
2. Meta ad copy driving traffic (message match)
3. Typeform URL
4. Specialization-specific eligibility (e.g. prenatal/postnatal certification + 1 year experience)
5. Teacher cards available (name, years of experience, a student quote each)?
6. Teacher testimonial videos available? (the shipped pages reuse one set)

## Section recipe

Section names refer to `LP_Section_Library.md`. Order follows the latest shipped copy doc.

| # | Section | Include | Category notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hero Section | Always | Button-only layout (library §1): "Apply Now" → Typeform. Not Combined Funnel Onboarding, so no name/WhatsApp form. Subheadline as dot-separated benefits ("Earn From Home · Flexible Schedule · Teach Worldwide") |
| 2 | Logo Mark | Always | Reuse the press logo row |
| 3 | Benefits Grid Section | Always | "Why Teach With MyYogaTeacher" — 6 value cards built from the value pillars |
| 4 | Benefit Section | Specialization pages | Eyebrow + headline + two short paragraphs, no bullet list |
| 5 | Expert Teachers | Specialization pages | "Meet Our [Specialization] Teachers" — experience line + a student quote per card |
| 6 | Video Testimonials | Always | "What Our Teachers Are Saying" — teacher voices |
| 7 | Eligibility Checklist | Always | Base criteria + specialization requirement + closing note (resume, training hours) |
| 8 | Easy to Get Started | Always | Apply & get screened → train & get ready → start teaching, plus closing line |
| 9 | FAQ Section | Always | See coverage below |
| 10 | Landing CTA | Always | "Apply Now" |

**What varies per specialization** (per the shipped doc): hero headline and subheadline, the
Benefit Section (specialization module), the Landing CTA headline, and the specialization line in
the Eligibility Checklist. Everything else carries over.

**FAQ coverage** (from the shipped page): do I need my own marketing; any fee to apply or train; how
and when I get paid; is this a fixed-income job; how many sessions a day; how long the process takes.

## Tone

Aspirational, mission-driven, still warm — never corporate or salesy (Brand Core §4).

## Category rules

- Earnings: marketplace framing always — never imply guaranteed income.
- No fees, no incentive programs (none exist).
- "Teacher", never "coach" — including teacher card titles and video labels.

## Optional add-ons

- Meta recruitment ad angles (earnings from home, flexibility, we bring the students, purpose)
- Page meta
