# Playbook — Workshop Landing Page (Free or Paid)

**Side:** Depends on the workshop — student-side (e.g. nutrition, wellness) or teacher-side (TTC —
teacher training certification). Confirm at intake.
**Track:** B — pure conversion (`MYT_Brand_Core.md` §9)
**Status:** ⚠️ Provisional — no shipped examples in `examples/workshop-lps/` yet. At first use, ask the
user for a real workshop LP. If they have none, generate and label the output provisional.
**Default CTA:** free → "Register for free"; paid → "Reserve your spot" (confirm) → registration or
payment destination (confirm URL)

## When this applies

A multi-session or deep-dive program with a defined curriculum — e.g. a nutrition workshop
(student-side) or a TTC workshop (teacher-side). Used less often than free events. Single free live
sessions and quarterly main events use `free-event-lp.md`.

## Category intake (on top of the shared intake)

1. Workshop topic and side (student or teacher)
2. Free or paid — if paid, the exact price, currency, and any payment plan
3. Structure: number of sessions/modules, duration, schedule, timezone
4. Format: live via Zoom? Recordings? Materials?
5. Host teacher(s) and credentials
6. Outcome — for TTC, the certification awarded, hours, and accreditation (never invent these)
7. Prerequisites or eligibility, if any
8. Seats or deadline (only if real)
9. Registration/payment destination
10. Past participant testimonials available?

## Tone

- Student-side: warm + genuine urgency/community layer (Brand Core §4).
- Teacher-side (TTC): aspirational, mission-driven, still warm — never corporate or salesy (Brand
  Core §4 teacher recruitment). Use `Teacher_Hiring_Context.md` only if the workshop connects to
  teaching on MYT — confirm that link rather than assuming it.

## Category rules

- Paid workshops show the price clearly — the "never mention price" rule is for paid-ad LPs only.
- Never invent certification hours, accreditation bodies, or refund terms — ask.
- Health-condition topics trigger the medical disclaimer (Brand Core §6).

## Optional add-ons

- Meta ad angles, email subject lines, page meta, Event/Course schema
