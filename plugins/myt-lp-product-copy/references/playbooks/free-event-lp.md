# Playbook — Free Event Landing Page

**Side:** Student · **Track:** B — pure conversion (`MYT_Brand_Core.md` §9)
**Status:** Calibrated on one shipped page — `examples/free-event-lps/yoga-for-sciatica-free-event.md`
(live page; Figma `Event-Page` node 2-2202 holds dummy copy). Ask for a quarterly main event example
when one comes up.
**Usage:** the most-used event category. Covers recurring weekly free sessions and quarterly main
events (e.g. International Yoga Day).
**CTA model:** the only real action is submitting the registration form (e.g. "Register Free").
Every other section CTA scrolls to the form (e.g. "Register Now").

## When this applies

A free, live student event — a recurring topic session ("Yoga for Sciatica", "Yoga for Back Pain")
or a quarterly main event. Multi-session programs with a curriculum use `workshop-lp.md`.

## Category intake (on top of the shared intake)

1. Event type: recurring weekly session or quarterly main event
2. Topic and the session's promise
3. Session length and format (live on Zoom? recorded or not?)
4. Level (e.g. all levels, beginner-friendly)
5. Host teacher — name, certifications, education, years of experience, credential tags. Never invent these.
6. Seat limit (only if real)
7. Social proof available (e.g. "100+ students reported relief", topic testimonials)
8. Form placement: in the hero (default) or as section 2
9. What happens after the session (e.g. an offer to explore 1-on-1 guidance) — used in the FAQ

Registration form fields are fixed — don't ask about or write copy for them.

## Section recipe

Section names refer to `LP_Section_Library.md`. Order follows the shipped page.

| # | Section | Include | Category notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Event Registration Hero | Always | Form panel in the hero by default; if the user wants the form as section 2, split it out directly below |
| 2 | Logo Mark | Always | Press logos |
| 3 | Benefit Section | Always | Event variant: "This is for you if..." — 4 audience-fit bullets, no intro, no CTA |
| 4 | Benefits Grid Section | Always | Event variant: "In [length], you will" — 6 outcome cards, no intro, no CTA |
| 5 | Meet {Teacher} | Always (one host) | Use Expert Teachers instead if several teachers lead |
| 6 | Text Testimonials | Always | Topic-relevant quotes (short is fine — 5–20 words), first names; supporting line on why live beats pre-recorded |
| 7 | FAQ Section | Always | Short answers; see coverage below |
| 8 | Landing CTA | Always | Short headline + one-line subtext; button scrolls to the form |

**Components to suggest when they fit** (from the library, not on the shipped page):
- Video Testimonials — for a quarterly main event with strong video stories
- Expert Teachers — if a main event has several teachers
- Easy to Get Started — if joining needs steps beyond the form (e.g. install Zoom, join link by email)
- Event Details Card — a main event with a fixed date/agenda instead of the "Pick a Time" picker

Propose these in the outline with a reason; don't add them silently.

**FAQ coverage** (from the shipped page): is it really free; will it be recorded; I'm a complete
beginner; what do I need; will my camera be on; what happens after the session.

## Word targets from the shipped page

| Element | Target |
|---|---|
| Landing CTA headline | ~5–6 words (e.g. "Free, Live, And Focused on You") |
| Landing CTA subtext | ~5–7 words (e.g. "Grab your mat and just show up!") |
| Testimonials headline | ~7–9 words (e.g. "Trusted by 3000+ Students to Relieve Sciatica Pain") |
| Testimonials supporting text | 2 sentences, ~20–25 words |
| FAQ question / answer | ~3–6 words / ~6–25 words |
| FAQ subheading | ~5 words (e.g. "Answers to the most common queries") |

Hero, Benefit, Benefits Grid, and Meet {Teacher} targets live in their library templates.

## Tone

Warm, encouraging, and specific to the topic, with a genuine urgency/community layer (Brand Core §4)
— real seat limits and real session times only. Plain, reassuring FAQ answers.

## Category rules

- State "free" clearly: hero feature items, the line under the form button, and the FAQ.
- Condition topics (sciatica, back pain) trigger the medical disclaimer (Brand Core §6).
- The shipped page says "You want a real teacher" — "real" is a banned term (Brand Core §3). Use
  "an expert teacher" instead.

## Optional add-ons

- Meta ad angles: the session's promise, who it's for, live vs recorded, seat/time urgency
- Email or WhatsApp reminder copy (subject lines / short reminders)
- Page meta + Event schema
