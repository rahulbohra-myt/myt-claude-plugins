# MyYogaTeacher — Company Context

## What the Company Does

MyYogaTeacher is an online yoga platform connecting certified Indian yoga teachers with students
primarily in the US, Canada, Australia, UAE, and Europe. Classes are delivered live via Zoom —
both 1-on-1 sessions and small group classes. The platform is accessible via web
(myyogateacher.com) and mobile app.

**Key differentiator:** Authentic traditional Indian yoga taught by India-certified teachers —
not westernized fitness yoga. Live, personal, and culturally rooted.

**Business model:** 3-day free trial → subscription (1-on-1 + group class options).

---

## Target Audience

### Primary: Indian Diaspora Abroad
- Living in US, UK, Canada, UAE
- Proud of Indian cultural roots; values authentic Indian teaching
- Ages 35–65 (some 65+)
- ~70% women, ~30% men
- Married with children; many in the "sandwich generation" (caring for kids + elderly parents)
- Busy professionals with significant time constraints

### Baseline Fitness & Psychology
- Predominantly sedentary (desk jobs)
- Not currently active; haven't exercised consistently in years
- Self-conscious in gyms/studios; intimidated by traditional fitness culture
- Past failed attempts (YouTube videos, gym memberships) have created hesitation
- Need a judgment-free, gentle re-entry into movement

### Pain Points
- Chronic stiffness, joint pain, low energy, posture problems from desk work
- Time poverty — juggling work, family, caregiving responsibilities
- Tried YouTube but lacked personalization and accountability
- Inconsistent results from past fitness attempts
- Can't commit to fixed schedules or studio commutes

### What They're Looking For
- Personalized guidance for their specific body and limitations
- Accountability without judgment
- Cultural trust — teachers who "get" Indian yoga philosophy
- Flexibility to practice around their schedule
- A gentle, safe way back to movement

---

## Brand Voice & Positioning

- **Tone:** Warm, encouraging, culturally resonant, judgment-free
- **Not:** Aggressive fitness marketing, westernized wellness buzzwords, intimidating
- **Core promise:** Authentic yoga from India's best teachers, personalized to your life
- **Trust signals:** India-certified teachers, live sessions (not recorded), free trial, community

### Key Messages
- "Someone who understands my body and my challenges"
- "Authentic yoga, not just fitness"
- "Guidance I can trust — from teachers who really know yoga"
- "Something that fits my schedule, not the other way around"
- "A gentle way back to movement after years of inactivity"
