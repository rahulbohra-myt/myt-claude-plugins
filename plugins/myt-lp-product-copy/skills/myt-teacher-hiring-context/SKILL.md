## Why Teach With MyYogaTeacher (Value Pillars)

1. **Earnings** — payment structure that values teaching expertise (see Earnings section)
2. **Flexibility** — teachers set their own availability/hours; no fixed shift
3. **Marketing & tech handled** — MYT brings the students and manages video conferencing, scheduling, and follow-ups; teachers focus on teaching, not logistics
4. **Community** — supportive teacher community oriented around collaboration and growth
5. **Global reach** — international visibility/recognition beyond what's possible via local studios alone
6. **Purpose** — framed around making the world healthier and happier through authentic yoga

Closing line from the deck: _"Your passion. Your expertise. Your schedule. Your global classroom."_

## Earnings

**Important framing rule:** This is a marketplace, not a job — no fixed or guaranteed income. Earnings are driven by supply and demand (teacher's own time/skill investment vs. student demand). Always pair any earnings figure with this context; never imply guaranteed income.

**Earnings growth curve** (avg / max, by tenure):

| Stage   | Avg. earnings | Max. earnings |
| ------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| Month 1 | Rs 3,500      | Rs 23,000     |
| Month 3 | Rs 20,000     | Rs 1,00,000   |
| Year 1  | Rs 45,000     | Rs 1,80,000   |
| Year 2  | Rs 60,000     | Rs 2,25,000   |

**Supporting claim:** Teachers who stay consistent and actively engage with students typically cross Rs 1,00,000 in earnings within their first year.

**Top-end data point:** Highest recorded teacher income was Rs 2,00,000 in a single month — use as an aspirational "top teachers" data point, not a typical-outcome claim.

## Eligibility Criteria

| Category       | Requirement                                                                  |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Education      | 300+ hours TTC, BSc, MSc, BNYS, or Diploma, plus 1+ year teaching experience |
| Communication  | English proficiency sufficient for international students                    |
| Internet       | Reliable, high-speed connection                                              |
| Equipment      | Laptop or tablet with a high-quality webcam                                  |
| Teaching space | Clean, quiet, bright, well-decorated home environment                        |

## Application & Onboarding Process

**1. Application**
Apply → Screening call → Demo session → 5-day training (includes credentials verification, class-flow best practices, profile creation)

**2. Getting Started**
Set availability → Students book sessions → Earn and grow (includes platform/policy intro, monthly payment setup)

**3. Ongoing Tools & Support**
Teacher app/dashboard for scheduling, marketing support (MYT brings the students), 24x7 live support

**Summary line:** "We bring students and manage the platform; you take the session."
